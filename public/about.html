Our Story

Passion, tradition, and culinary excellence since 2009

Welcome to Bistrot Des Tournelles

Located in the heart of the historic Marais district, Bistrot Des Tournelles has been serving authentic French cuisine to locals and travelers alike since 2009.

Our chef, trained in the finest culinary schools of Lyon and Paris, brings decades of experience and an unwavering passion for traditional French cooking. Every dish is prepared with the freshest seasonal ingredients sourced from local markets and trusted producers.

We believe in the art of slow food – taking the time to prepare each dish with care, honoring centuries-old recipes while adding our own contemporary touch.
